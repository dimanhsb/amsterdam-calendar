# FreeDam Tours

Official operator page: <https://freedamtours.com/>. The overview and FAQ state that the public “Absolutely Amsterdam” tour runs daily at 10:30 and 14:00, plus 17:30 Sunday–Friday, lasts about 2.5 hours, and is in English. The FAQ says reservations are needed to guarantee a place, public tour groups have up to 15 people, and free-tour parties are limited to five.

Price wording needs care: the operator describes the public tour as free, says the guide tip is voluntary, and describes €15–€25 as a common amount from guests. Keep the price type `tip-based`, amount null; do not call it unconditionally free. Private Dutch/German tours are different offers and should not be merged with the public English tour.
