# Pakhuis de Zwijger source guide

## Entry URLs

- Month agenda: https://dezwijger.nl/agenda/start/2026/11
- Example participatory event: https://dezwijger.nl/programma/memory-ball-a-hands-on-memory-workshop
- Example public jury: https://dezwijger.nl/programma/grenzen-zegen-of-vloek
- Venue information appears at the bottom of the agenda: Piet Heinkade 179, 1019 HC Amsterdam.

## Observed navigation and extraction

1. Open the month-specific agenda and confirm the selected month heading.
2. Scan each individual event card for exact date, start, room, and any “en english” label.
3. Open the event detail page linked by the card. The detail page supplies format language, exact ticket price and booking link.
4. Record the audience interaction exactly as described. “Interactive”/“audience has an active role” supports active participation; a question period alone is weaker. Do not infer icebreakers, solo welcome, end time or community continuity.

Example: the November agenda card for “Memory Ball: A hands-on memory workshop” gives Tue 10 Nov, 19:30, Studio and English. Its detail page says €11 and describes adding an Amsterdam memory to a shared archive and making a take-home object. The event record uses `2026-11-10T19:30:00+01:00`, leaves end null, and describes the social potential as an assessment rather than organizer promise.

## Costs, booking and clock changes

Use the dated event page's stated ticket price. The visible ticket value is the regular displayed ticket; do not assume membership or concession discounts unless that exact page lists them. Reserve through the official organizer page; do not claim remaining seats unless checked. Use Europe/Amsterdam +02:00 through 24 October 2026 and +01:00 from 25 October onward. Never copy summertime offsets into November.

## Stopping rule and inexpensive repeat workflow

Stop on a candidate when the official page lacks an exact eligible start date/time, does not establish Amsterdam location, or falls outside the requested time window. A month agenda plus exact event page is enough; do not inspect generic weekly series or paid booking flows. For a replay, open the month agenda, follow promising “workshop”, “interactive”, “audience role” and discussion cards, then record only the statements visible on the event page. Keep price unknown if absent.
