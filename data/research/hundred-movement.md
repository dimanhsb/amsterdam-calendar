# Additional movement listings

Checked 2026-10-18. This batch adds 43 individually published, date-specific class listings: 30 Amsterdam Dance Centre sessions and 13 EQUAL Linnaeusstraat sessions. Every date and start time comes from a public booking-calendar row selected in the rendered schedule; no weekly recurrence was extrapolated.

ADC entries span Oct 26–Nov 28, 2026 and include only classes labelled beginner, basic, open level, or all levels. Intermediate and advanced listings were excluded. The adult FAQ lists a €22.50 single class without membership.

EQUAL entries cover Oct 20–Nov 1, 2026, the date range selected in its public calendar. The chosen Hot Fusion, Hot Pilates, and Core Flow classes are identified as beginner suitable by their session descriptions or EQUAL’s FAQ. A single-class card costs €20; heated classes require a towel or €2.50 rental.

In the EQUAL calendar, Amsterdam GMT+2 appeared through Oct 24 and GMT+1 from Oct 25, so timestamps use the appropriate offset. All rows are new relative to `data/events.json` as checked. Availability was not checked beyond seeing the public signup button.
