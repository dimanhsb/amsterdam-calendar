# Fitness and climbing drop-in research

Checked 2026-10-18. This is a separate research batch; no canonical app/calendar files were changed.

Twelve current public one-visit offers have direct prices and first-visit caveats in `deep-fitness.json`. Best novice bouldering option: Beta’s €19.90 Intro to Bouldering is a single 1–1.5 hour lesson with shoes and entry included and no prior experience required. Beest and Monk offer lower-cost self-directed day entry with safety orientations. For a low-impact visit, municipal single swim tickets cost €6.40; Noorderparkbad also lists a 45-minute Aquasport class for €9.60.

Prices were verified from the public venue pages where available. Beest’s tariff lists first-visit entry €19.70, registered entry €14.70, and registration/member card €5; the arithmetic indicates €19.70 is the combined first-visit total (not plus another €5), though the page does not explicitly say inclusive. Shoes and chalk are separate. Klimmuur Centraal’s €18.70 is only an indicative third-party listing; current primary rope-climbing tariff and rental prices were not found, and Beest’s tariff is explicitly for bouldering. Vondelgym’s €19.50 day pass covers one class or self-training. Het Gymlokaal’s €17.50 credit is explicitly good for one class or one gym-facility visit. Eastbound’s single lesson starts at €16.50; Boogieland’s single group lesson is €15, with a specific beginner caveat.

Monk’s official page advertises LOCO #1 on Nov 14 with new boulders and a DJ, but publishes no start time. Beta’s intro calendar is public, but no specific date/time slot was selected, so the batch contains no dated event rows.
